Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a refund for a game?

You can request a refund for any game within 14 days of purchase and with less than 2 hours of playtime. Go to your purchase history and click "Request Refund" for eligible items.

Why is my game downloading slowly?

Slow downloads can be caused by network congestion, server load, or your internet connection. Try changing your download region in Steam settings or check your network connection.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Go to Steam Guard in your account settings and select "Enable Steam Guard". You can choose between email authentication or the Steam Guard mobile app for enhanced security.

Can I play games offline?

Yes, many games can be played offline. Enable offline mode in Steam settings, and make sure to launch your games at least once while online to verify ownership.

How do I share games with family members?

Use Family Sharing to allow family members to play your games. Go to Family in Steam settings and authorize their accounts. They can then access your shared library.
