About Steam

Revolutionizing digital distribution since 2003

Our Mission

Steam is the ultimate destination for playing, discussing, and creating games. We're committed to bringing the best gaming experiences to players worldwide through innovative technology, community features, and an extensive library of games.

What We Do

Steam provides a comprehensive platform for PC gaming, offering digital distribution, social networking, matchmaking, and anti-cheat services. We serve both players and developers, creating an ecosystem that benefits the entire gaming community.

Our History

2003

Steam Launches

Steam was launched by Valve Corporation as a way to automatically update games and provide anti-cheat measures for multiplayer games.

2005

Digital Distribution

Steam expanded beyond Valve games to become a full digital distribution platform for third-party games.

2008

Community Features

Steam Community launched, adding friend lists, groups, and user-generated content features.

2012

Steam Greenlight

Steam Greenlight was introduced, allowing the community to vote on which games should be published on Steam.

2015

Steam Direct

Steam Direct replaced Greenlight, making it easier for developers to publish games on the platform.

2020

Remote Play Together

Steam introduced Remote Play Together, allowing friends to join local multiplayer games over the internet.

2024

Steam Deck

Steam continues to innovate with handheld gaming devices and expanded platform features.
